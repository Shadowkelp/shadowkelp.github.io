📐 How to Use MagnaBuild 3D

🖱️ Camera Controls (Desktop)

  • Right-click + drag - Orbit camera
  • Scroll wheel - Zoom in/out
  • Middle-click + drag or Space + drag - Pan

📱 Camera Controls (Mobile)

  • One finger on empty space - Orbit
  • Pinch - Zoom
  • Two fingers - Pan

🧱 Building

  • Press 1-4 or tap icons to select piece type
  • Click ADD to spawn a new tile
  • Click/tap a tile to select it
  • Drag selected tile to move it
  • Q/E - Rotate 15° left/right
  • Use Tilt buttons for wall angles (0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°)
  • Adjust Height slider to raise tiles

🧲 Magnetic Snapping

  • Drag tiles near each other to see green snap preview
  • Release to snap edges together
  • Edges must have matching magnet counts (2↔2, 4↔4)
  • Works for flat, angled, and wall connections!

⚡ Quick Actions

  • Delete or Backspace - Remove selected tile
  • D - Duplicate selected tile
  • G - Toggle grid
